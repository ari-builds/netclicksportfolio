Abstract

We present Hydra-7B, an open-weight 3D model of a water bottle for rendering, simulation, and gloriously unnecessary research. Hydra-7B faithfully reproduces key hydration behaviors — desk protection, perfect cylindrical symmetry, and passive thermal moderation under everyday beverage conditions. Released in clean OBJ format with baseline results on our own DeskBench (a standardized evaluation suite conducted on a single desk and very possibly rigged by us). Limitations include heavy dependency on gravity, liquids, and human deployment.
